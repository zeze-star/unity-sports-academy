# UNITY SPORTS ACADEMY — DESIGN SYSTEM & AESTHETIC GUIDE

## 1. VISION & MOOD
A fusion of high-performance sports energy and elite luxury.
- **Aesthetic Pillars**: Apple (Minimalism), Nike (Athletic Energy), Porsche (Luxury), Adidas (Campaign Storytelling).
- **Style**: Liquid Glassmorphism, Cinematic Motion, Editorial Typography.
- **Tone**: Premium, Fast, Emotional, Motivational, Confident, Immersive.

---

## 2. COLOR PALETTE

- **Deep Forest Green**: `#0D2B1E` (The foundation, stability, and growth)

- Frosted glass overlays (Glassmorphism)

---

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM
- **Headings**: Clash Display / Satoshi / General Sans / Neue Montreal
  - *Characteristics*: Bold, Cinematic, Oversized, Tight line-height, Elegant spacing.
- **Body**: Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans
  - *Characteristics*: High readability, clean visual hierarchy, dynamic scaling.

### Hero Messaging Examples
- “Every Dream Begins With One Kick.”
- “Developing Kenya’s Next Football Legends.”
- “Train Like Champions. Compete With Purpose.”

---

## 4. UI COMPONENTS & PRINCIPLES
### Principles
- **Dimensionality**: Use layers, blur, and depth (Glassmorphism).
- **Interactivity**: Everything should feel alive (Hover states, magnetic buttons, micro-interactions).
- **Handcrafted**: No generic grids; asymmetrical layouts and premium spacing.

### Key Components
- **Liquid Glass Cards**: Smooth corners, subtle glow, 3D tilt on hover.
- **Magnetic Buttons**: Elements that subtly follow the cursor.
- **Floating UI Layers**: Stats and widgets that feel like they are floating over cinematic backgrounds.

---

## 5. MOTION & ANIMATION (The "Alive" System)
- **Frameworks**: Framer Motion, GSAP, Lenis Smooth Scroll.
- **Style**: Slow elegant easing, natural movement physics, blur-to-focus transitions.
- **Key Effects**:
  - Scroll-trigger reveals (Content entering gracefully).
  - Staggered text reveals (Character/word by word).
  - Parallax imagery (Depth during scroll).
  - Image scale hover effects (Cinematic zoom).

---

## 6. PAGE-SPECIFIC DESIGN DIRECTIONS
### Homepage
- **Hero**: Fullscreen cinematic field, glass stat widgets, massive typography.
- **Programs**: Interactive "campaign tiles" with 3D tilt and glow.
- **Match Centre**: Modern sports broadcast aesthetic (ESPN/FIFA style).
### About

### Programs / Schedule / Pricing
